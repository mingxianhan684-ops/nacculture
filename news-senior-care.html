Senior Care Program

敬老爱老，温暖相伴

Supporting dignity, companionship, health, and community connections for seniors.

Caring for seniors is not only an act of kindness—it is a commitment to dignity, respect, companionship, and community responsibility.

A community activity supporting companionship and care for seniors.

Caring for the People Who Built Our Communities

Older adults have contributed their knowledge, experience, work, and love to their families and communities. As people grow older, they may face loneliness, limited mobility, health concerns, language barriers, and difficulty accessing community resources.

The North America Chinese Culture Foundation’s Senior Care Program seeks to build meaningful connections with seniors and help them feel respected, valued, and included in community life.

How the Program Supports Seniors

Companionship

Organizing visits, conversations, celebrations, and activities that help reduce loneliness and strengthen human connection.

Community Resources

Helping seniors and families learn about useful community services, support programs, and local resources.

Cultural Connection

Creating opportunities for seniors to participate in cultural activities and share their stories, traditions, and experience.

Respect and Dignity

Promoting a community culture that values older adults and recognizes their lifelong contributions.

Bringing Generations Together

Senior care becomes more meaningful when different generations participate together. Volunteers, students, families, professionals, and community organizations can all help create a stronger network of respect and companionship.

Activities may include senior visits, cultural celebrations, friendly conversations, community gatherings, resource sharing, and volunteer service. The foundation will continue to share photographs and reports as future senior care activities are organized.

Building a More Caring Community

The Senior Care Program is part of NACCF’s broader commitment to community service. Our goal is to help seniors remain connected to the people and culture around them while encouraging families and volunteers to take an active role in caring for older community members.

Your Support Can Bring Warmth and Companionship

Donations can help support senior activities, community outreach, cultural programs, volunteer services, and other resources that improve the lives of older adults.
