紧急援助基金

Emergency Relief Fund

在最困难的时刻，把及时的帮助送到最需要的人身边。
Deliver timely assistance to those who need it most during life's most difficult moments.

困难来临时，帮助不能迟到
Help Should Never Arrive Too Late

生活中的危机常常来得突然：疾病、失业、意外事故、自然灾害、家庭变故，都可能让一个家庭瞬间陷入困境。
Life crises often arrive without warning. Illness, unemployment, accidents, natural disasters and family emergencies can place a family in immediate hardship.

北美中华文化基金会设立紧急援助基金，是希望在突发困难出现时，为有需要的个人和家庭提供及时、温暖、实际的帮助。
The North America Chinese Culture Foundation established the Emergency Relief Fund to provide timely, compassionate and practical support to individuals and families facing unexpected hardship.

紧急援助不是简单的救济，而是在一个人最无助的时候，告诉他：你不是一个人。
Emergency relief is more than temporary assistance. It tells people during their most vulnerable moments that they are not alone.

为什么这个项目重要？

Why this program matters

因为危机不会等待
Because Emergencies Cannot Wait

当困难突然发生时，及时的援助往往比长期承诺更重要。紧急援助可以帮助家庭渡过最危险的时刻。
When hardship strikes suddenly, immediate assistance is often more important than future promises. Emergency relief helps families survive their most difficult moments.

因为一份帮助能稳定一个家庭
Because One Helping Hand Can Stabilize a Family

一次食品援助、一笔临时费用、一份医疗支持，都可能避免一个家庭陷入更严重的困境。
Food assistance, temporary financial help or medical support may prevent a family from falling into a more serious crisis.

因为善意就是安全网
Because Kindness Is a Safety Net

一个有爱心的社区，应该在成员最脆弱的时候提供支持。紧急援助基金就是这样的安全网。
A compassionate community supports its members when they are most vulnerable. The Emergency Relief Fund serves as that essential safety net.
