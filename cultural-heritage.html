文化传承计划

Cultural Heritage Program

守护文化，温暖人心。让中华文化在北美社区继续传承与发光。

Preserving heritage and bringing people together, so Chinese culture may continue to flourish throughout North American communities.

文化，是一个民族的根

Culture Is the Root of a People

文化不仅是历史，更是一个民族的记忆、精神与身份。

Culture is more than history. It carries the memory, spirit and identity of a people.

语言、书法、瓷器、绘画、戏曲、节日、书籍和礼仪，都承载着中华文明数千年的智慧。

Language, calligraphy, ceramics, painting, traditional opera, festivals, literature and customs all carry the wisdom of thousands of years of Chinese civilization.

北美中华文化基金会希望通过文化传承计划，在北美社区推广中华优秀传统文化， 促进不同族裔、不同文化之间的理解与交流。

Through the Cultural Heritage Program, the North America Chinese Culture Foundation promotes outstanding Chinese traditions in North American communities and encourages understanding and exchange among people of different cultures and backgrounds.

文化传承不是停留在过去，而是让下一代在新的土地上依然知道自己从哪里来， 也知道未来可以走向哪里。

Cultural preservation does not mean remaining in the past. It helps future generations understand where they come from while building confidence in where they can go.

为什么这个项目重要？

Why This Program Matters

因为文化不能断代

Culture Must Be Passed to Future Generations

如果文化失去了传承，下一代将失去根。文化教育让孩子们理解语言、历史和身份。

Without cultural continuity, future generations may lose their connection to their roots. Cultural education helps young people understand their language, history and identity.

因为交流能减少隔阂

Cultural Exchange Builds Understanding

文化交流能够让不同背景的人彼此理解，减少偏见，建立尊重与友谊。

Cultural exchange helps people from different backgrounds understand one another, reduce prejudice and build mutual respect and friendship.

因为艺术连接心灵

Art Connects People Across Cultures

书法、瓷器、音乐、戏曲和文学能够跨越语言，让人们感受共同的人性与美。

Calligraphy, ceramics, music, traditional opera and literature can cross language barriers and help people experience our shared humanity and appreciation of beauty.
