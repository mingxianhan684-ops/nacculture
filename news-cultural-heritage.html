Keeping Cultural Heritage Alive

Chinese culture carries thousands of years of history, art, music, literature, philosophy, traditions, and family values. These cultural treasures continue to provide knowledge and inspiration to people around the world.

The North America Chinese Culture Foundation’s Cultural Heritage Program supports activities that introduce traditional culture to younger generations while building bridges between Chinese communities and the wider public.

Our Cultural Priorities

Traditional Arts

Supporting activities involving music, dance, painting, calligraphy, literature, crafts, and other forms of cultural expression.

Cultural Education

Helping children, students, and community members learn about Chinese history, language, traditions, and values.

Community Celebrations

Encouraging cultural festivals, exhibitions, performances, lectures, and community gatherings.

Cross-Cultural Understanding

Building opportunities for people from different backgrounds to learn, communicate, and develop mutual respect.

Connecting Generations Through Culture

Cultural heritage becomes stronger when knowledge is shared between generations. Seniors and experienced cultural practitioners can pass on stories and traditions, while young people can use new ideas and technology to help culture reach wider audiences.

The foundation welcomes artists, educators, students, cultural organizations, community groups, and volunteers who would like to participate in future cultural activities.

Culture as a Bridge Between Communities

Cultural programs do more than preserve the past. They create conversations in the present and help build a more respectful future. Through cultural exchange, people can recognize shared values while appreciating the traditions that make each community unique.
