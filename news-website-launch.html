Official Website Launch

北美中华文化基金会官方网站正式上线

June 2026 · Houston, Texas

The North America Chinese Culture Foundation is pleased to announce the official launch of its website, creating a new digital home for our programs, community stories, volunteer opportunities, and charitable initiatives.

A New Digital Home for Our Mission

The launch of the NACCF website represents an important step in strengthening communication between the foundation and the communities we serve. Through this platform, visitors can learn more about our mission, explore our charitable programs, follow foundation news, and discover ways to participate.

Our website brings together information about education support, senior care, cultural heritage, community service, animal rescue, and emergency relief. Each program reflects our commitment to compassion, dignity, cultural understanding, and responsible community service.

Connecting People, Programs, and Communities

The website is designed to make foundation information clear and accessible. Community members can follow program updates, read stories of kindness, learn about volunteer opportunities, and contact the foundation regarding partnerships and charitable support.

Program Information

Learn about NACCF programs serving students, seniors, families, culture, animals, and local communities.

News and Community Stories

Follow foundation activities, volunteer service, community events, and stories of compassion.

Volunteer Opportunities

Discover ways to contribute your time, knowledge, professional skills, and community experience.

Support Our Work

Learn how charitable contributions can help strengthen the foundation’s programs and community impact.

Looking Ahead

The official website will continue to grow as the foundation develops new activities and partnerships. Future updates will include additional program reports, event photographs, volunteer stories, community announcements, and information about upcoming charitable initiatives.

We welcome community members, schools, businesses, cultural organizations, volunteers, donors, and partner organizations to follow our work and help us build a more caring and connected community.
