老年护理项目

Senior Care Program

今天的老人，就是明天的我们。让陪伴、尊严与关爱，重新回到每一位长者身边。

Today’s seniors reflect our own future. Let companionship, dignity and compassionate care return to every older adult in our community.

让晚年不再孤单

Helping Seniors Live with Companionship and Dignity

许多老人生活在孤独、疾病、语言障碍和经济压力之中。 他们需要的不只是物质帮助，更需要陪伴、尊重和被看见。

Many older adults live with loneliness, illness, language barriers and financial hardship. They need more than material assistance; they also need companionship, respect and the reassurance that they have not been forgotten.

北美中华文化基金会通过老年护理项目，为社区长者提供节日关怀、 生活慰问、陪伴探访、健康支持和紧急援助。

Through the Senior Care Program, the North America Chinese Culture Foundation provides holiday care, wellness visits, companionship, health-resource assistance and emergency support for seniors in our community.

我们相信，一个真正温暖的社区，不会忘记曾经为家庭和社会付出一生的老人。

We believe a truly compassionate community never forgets the older adults who devoted their lives to their families and to society.

👴 长者探访

Senior Visits and Companionship

组织志愿者探访独居老人，提供陪伴、问候和基本生活协助。

Organizing volunteer visits for seniors who live alone and providing companionship, friendly conversation and basic assistance.

🎁 节日关怀包

Holiday Care Packages

为老人准备食品、生活用品、节日礼品和温暖问候。

Preparing food, household necessities, holiday gifts and caring messages for seniors during important holidays.

🏥 健康支持

Health and Wellness Support

协助老人获得健康信息、社区资源和必要的医疗转介。

Helping seniors access health information, community services and appropriate referrals to medical resources.

🥘 餐食援助

Meal and Nutrition Assistance

支持困难老人获得日常餐食、营养补给和生活必需品。

Helping seniors facing hardship obtain meals, nutritional support and essential household supplies.

🚗 出行协助

Transportation Assistance

在能力范围内帮助老人解决就医、购物和参加社区活动的交通问题。

Assisting seniors, when possible, with transportation for medical visits, grocery shopping and community activities.

❤️ 紧急援助

Emergency Assistance

为突发困难、疾病或生活危机中的老人提供及时帮助。

Providing timely support to seniors facing illness, unexpected hardship or an urgent personal crisis.

为什么这个项目重要？

Why This Program Matters

每一位长者都值得被尊重、被关心、被温柔地对待。
Every senior deserves respect, care and compassionate support.

因为老人需要被看见

Seniors Deserve to Be Seen and Remembered

许多老人沉默地承受孤独和困难。一次探访、一通电话、一份关怀， 都可能成为他们生活里的光。

Many seniors quietly endure loneliness and hardship. A personal visit, a phone call or a thoughtful act of care can bring light and comfort into their lives.

因为尊严不该被遗忘

Dignity Should Never Be Forgotten

关爱老人，不是施舍，而是对生命经验、家庭贡献和社会记忆的尊重。

Caring for seniors is not an act of charity alone. It is an expression of respect for their life experience, family contributions and place in our shared community history.

因为每个人都会老去

Everyone Will Grow Older

今天我们如何对待老人，就是未来社会如何对待我们的样子。

The way we treat older adults today helps shape the kind of society that will care for all of us in the future.
