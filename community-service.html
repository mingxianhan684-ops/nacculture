社区关爱计划

Community Service Program

让社区多一份温暖，让困难家庭多一份希望。

Bringing greater warmth to our communities and renewed hope to families facing hardship.

一个社区的温度，来自每一次帮助

The Warmth of a Community Begins with Every Act of Help

社区不是冷冰冰的街道和房屋，而是人与人之间的关怀、支持和连接。

A community is more than streets and buildings. It is built through care, support and meaningful connections between people.

在北美生活的家庭中，有些人正在经历经济压力、语言障碍、孤独、 疾病、失业或突发困难。

Some families living in North America are facing financial pressure, language barriers, loneliness, illness, unemployment or unexpected hardship.

北美中华文化基金会希望通过社区关爱计划，为有需要的家庭、 青少年、老人和弱势群体提供及时支持。

Through the Community Service Program, the North America Chinese Culture Foundation seeks to provide timely support to families, young people, seniors and vulnerable members of the community.

我们相信，一个真正强大的社区，是在有人遇到困难时， 仍然有人愿意伸出手。

We believe a truly strong community is one in which someone is always willing to extend a helping hand when another person faces difficulty.

为什么这个项目重要？

Why This Program Matters

社区关爱能够帮助人们渡过困难，也能建立更加紧密和温暖的社会联系
Community care helps people overcome hardship while building stronger and more compassionate connections

因为困难常常来得突然

Hardship Can Happen Without Warning

一次失业、一场疾病、一张账单，都可能让一个家庭陷入困境。 社区支持可以帮助他们重新站起来。

A job loss, an illness or an unexpected bill can suddenly place a family in crisis. Community support can help them recover and move forward again.

因为新移民需要连接

Newcomers Need Community Connections

很多新移民家庭面对语言、文化和生活适应压力， 需要一个可信赖的社区支持网络。

Many newly arrived immigrant families face language, cultural and daily-life adjustment challenges. They need a trusted community support network.

因为善意会传递

Kindness Creates a Lasting Cycle

当一个人被帮助，他也更可能在未来帮助别人。 社区公益创造的是长期的温暖循环。

When a person receives help, they are more likely to help someone else in the future. Community service creates a lasting cycle of kindness and care.
