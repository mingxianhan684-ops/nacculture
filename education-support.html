助学基金

Education Support Fund

帮助经济困难学生继续读书，让每一个孩子都有机会通过教育改变未来。

Helping students in financial need continue their education and giving every child an opportunity to build a better future through learning.

教育，是改变命运的机会

Education Is an Opportunity to Change Lives

有些学生不是不努力，而是缺少继续前进的条件。

Some students do not lack determination. They simply lack the resources and opportunities needed to continue moving forward.

他们可能需要一本教材、一台电脑、一笔考试费、一份学费帮助， 或者一次被看见、被支持的机会。

They may need a textbook, a computer, assistance with examination or tuition fees, or simply an opportunity to be seen, encouraged and supported.

北美中华文化基金会设立助学基金，是希望帮助经济困难学生继续完成学业， 获得更加公平的发展机会。

The North America Chinese Culture Foundation established the Education Support Fund to help students facing financial hardship continue their studies and gain fairer opportunities for personal and academic development.

帮助一个学生，不只是帮助一个人，也是在帮助一个家庭看见更有希望的未来。

Supporting one student does more than change one life. It helps an entire family see a more hopeful future.

为什么这个项目重要？

Why This Program Matters

教育资源的差距，不应成为孩子追求梦想的障碍。
A lack of resources should never prevent a child from pursuing a dream.

因为贫困不该阻挡梦想

Poverty Should Not Stand in the Way of a Dream

很多学生拥有能力和梦想，却因为经济压力无法获得足够的学习资源。 一次帮助，可能成为他们继续前进的重要力量。

Many students have talent and ambition but cannot access sufficient educational resources because of financial hardship. A single act of support may give them the strength to continue.

因为教育改变家庭

Education Can Transform an Entire Family

一个学生获得教育机会，往往会影响整个家庭的未来。 教育是最长期、最有价值的公益投资之一。

When one student gains access to education, the benefits often extend to the entire family. Education is one of the most valuable and long-lasting forms of charitable investment.

因为每一份支持都有意义

Every Contribution Can Make a Difference

无论是一笔奖学金、一套教材，还是一台电脑， 都可能成为孩子人生道路上的关键转折点。

Whether it provides a scholarship, a set of textbooks or a computer, every contribution can become a turning point in a young person’s life.

一个值得支持的梦想

A Dream Worth Supporting

每一个孩子都值得拥有梦想，也值得拥有追求梦想的机会。
Every child deserves both a dream and the opportunity to pursue it.

每一个孩子都值得拥有梦想，也值得拥有追求梦想的机会。

Every child deserves to have a dream and a fair opportunity to work toward it.

对于一些家庭来说，一台电脑可能是一笔沉重的开支；一本教材可能需要反复借阅； 一次培训机会可能因为经济原因而错过。

For some families, purchasing a computer may be a serious financial burden. A student may have to repeatedly borrow textbooks, or miss an important training opportunity because the family cannot afford it.

然而，对学生而言，这些资源可能成为他们进入大学、获得奖学金、学习新技能， 以及改变人生道路的重要起点。

Yet these resources may become the starting point that helps a student enter college, earn a scholarship, develop new skills and change the direction of their life.

助学基金的意义，就是在孩子最需要的时候，给他们一份看得见、用得上的支持。

The purpose of the Education Support Fund is to provide meaningful, practical assistance at the moment a student needs it most.
