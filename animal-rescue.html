一个生命，也值得被守护
Every Life Deserves Protection

在城市的角落里，有许多流浪猫狗正在经历饥饿、疾病、受伤和寒冷。
In the hidden corners of our cities, many stray cats and dogs are suffering from hunger, illness, injury and cold weather.

它们不会说话，但它们同样会疼痛、会害怕，也会渴望被看见、被照顾、被温柔对待。
They cannot speak, but they still feel pain and fear. They also long to be seen, cared for and treated with compassion.

北美中华文化基金会设立动物救助基金，希望为更多流浪动物提供医疗救助、食物补给、临时寄养、绝育支持和领养机会。
The North America Chinese Culture Foundation established the Animal Rescue Fund to provide more homeless animals with medical treatment, food, temporary foster care, spay and neuter support and adoption opportunities.

您的每一次捐赠，都可能让一个受伤的生命重新获得希望。
Every donation you make may give an injured animal a new chance and renewed hope.

您的捐款将用于

Your donation will support animal rescue work

🏥 医疗救助
Medical Care

帮助受伤、患病的流浪动物获得检查、治疗、手术和药物支持。
Helping injured and sick homeless animals receive examinations, medical treatment, surgery and necessary medication.

🍖 食物补给
Food Assistance

为流浪猫狗提供基础食物、饮水和临时救助物资。
Providing homeless cats and dogs with essential food, clean water and temporary relief supplies.

🏠 临时寄养
Temporary Foster Care

支持寄养家庭、临时庇护和安全安置，避免动物继续流浪。
Supporting foster families, temporary shelters and safe placement so rescued animals do not have to remain on the streets.

🐾 绝育支持
Spay and Neuter Support

帮助控制流浪动物数量，减少更多生命在街头受苦。
Helping control the homeless animal population and preventing more animals from suffering on the streets.

❤️ 爱心领养
Compassionate Adoption

推动领养宣传，帮助被救助动物找到长期、稳定、温暖的家庭。
Promoting adoption and helping rescued animals find permanent, stable and loving homes.

📚 社区教育
Community Education

开展动物保护、责任养宠、绝育意识和善待生命的公益宣传。
Providing public education about animal protection, responsible pet ownership, spay and neuter awareness and compassionate treatment of all living beings.

为什么动物救助重要？

Why animal rescue matters

因为生命不该被遗弃
Because No Life Should Be Abandoned

许多流浪动物曾经也有家。救助它们，是对生命最基本的尊重。
Many homeless animals once had families and homes. Rescuing them is one of the most basic ways to show respect for life.

因为救助也是社区文明
Animal Rescue Reflects a Caring Community

善待动物的社区，往往也更懂得关心弱者、尊重生命、传递温暖。
A community that treats animals with kindness is also more likely to protect vulnerable lives, respect others and spread compassion.

因为一次帮助可以改变一生
One Act of Help Can Change an Entire Life

一顿饭、一针疫苗、一次手术、一个领养机会，都可能改变一个生命的结局。
A meal, a vaccination, a medical procedure or an adoption opportunity can completely change the future of an animal.

Every Life Deserves Love

每一个生命都值得被爱。您的善意，不只是一次捐赠，而是一次重新点亮生命的机会。
Every life deserves love. Your kindness is more than a donation—it is an opportunity to bring hope and light back into a life.
